Read before you compete

Rules & regulations

Please go through these carefully before your scheduled event.

Camera setup

  • Your camera must stay stable for the entire solve.
  • Your cube, cube cover, timer, and both hands must be clearly visible at all times.
  • The whole solving area must stay inside the camera frame throughout the attempt.

Before the solve

  • Apply the scramble exactly as provided.
  • Scrambles are sent one at a time in the event call chat.
  • Solve only the scramble currently given to you.
  • After scrambling, show a side of the cube clearly to the camera, then wait for the organizer to instruct you to cover it.

Starting the attempt

  • The organizer will count "1...2...3...Start."
  • A 15-second inspection begins right after "Start."
  • Complete your solve, then show your timer clearly to the camera so the result is visible.

Attendance & conduct

  • Join the call at your allotted time — late arrivals may be marked DNS.
  • No outside assistance is allowed during an attempt.
  • Unfair practice or rule violations can lead to disqualification.

Registration & refunds

  • Registration fees are non-refundable once registration is complete.
  • Please double-check your event selections before paying.
